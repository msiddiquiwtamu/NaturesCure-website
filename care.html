Unani medicine

Nature’s Cure is a consultation clinic in Greenpark, Manjur Garhi, Aligarh, with a focus on Unani medicine. Dr. Siddiqui’s qualifications in Ilmul Advia and Moalejat provide the academic foundation for his work.

A consultation is an opportunity to discuss your health concerns, relevant medical history, and questions about the approach. Please contact the clinic to find out whether it is suitable for the concern you would like to discuss.

Ilaj-bit-Tadbeer

Ilaj-bit-Tadbeer, also called regimenal therapy, is Dr. Siddiqui’s particular area of academic and clinical interest. His work has included the development of this discipline through teaching, research, and departmental leadership at Aligarh Muslim University.

Specific treatment options, their suitability, and any next steps should be discussed directly with the doctor during a consultation.

Preparing for a consultation

Call before visiting to confirm availability, timings, and consultation fees. Bring relevant medical reports, details of previous treatment, and a list of medicines or supplements you currently take.

Let the doctor know about any existing conditions, allergies, or ongoing care. Do not stop or change prescribed medicines without speaking to your treating clinician.
